Frequently Asked Questions About Our Machinery

Fly ash bricks are eco-friendly building materials manufactured using industrial fly ash, sand/stone dust, lime, and cement under high hydraulic compaction pressure. They provide uniform shape, lower mortar consumption, and superior compressive strength compared to traditional clay red bricks.

Fully automatic PLC machines feature automated pan mixers, conveyor belts, automated hydraulic press feeding, and automatic pallet stacking, delivering 10,000+ bricks/day with minimal labor.
