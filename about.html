Who We Are

Pioneering Sustainable Construction Solutions

Founded by Jayesh Panchal in 2022, Jee Engineers brings together nearly two decades of deep industry expertise. Having worked in the brick machinery manufacturing sector since 2006, Mr. Panchal established Jee Engineers in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, to deliver heavy-duty, high-compaction fly ash brick making machines engineered for maximum durability, minimal power consumption, and peak daily productivity.

With over 18 years of hands-on technical experience in hydraulic press automation and structural fabrication, our leadership has helped 500+ clients launch profitable brick manufacturing plants across India. Our commitment to precision engineering, eco-friendly waste utilization, and complete 24/7 turnkey field support sets us apart as an industry leader.

Core Engineering Principles

Built on Durability, Precision & Eco-Innovation

Our four core engineering pillars drive the design, manufacturing, and long-term reliability of every machine that leaves our factory floor.

Heavy-Duty Durability

Fabricated with thick high-tensile steel plates, reinforced columns, and branded hydraulic valves to withstand 24/7 heavy commercial operation for 20+ years.

Peak Energy Efficiency

Engineered with custom hydraulic power packs and high-power-factor motors that optimize cycle times and cut electricity consumption per brick by up to 30%.

Eco-Green Innovation

Designed specifically for maximum industrial waste utilization—converting thermal power station fly ash, lime sludge, and slag into high-strength eco bricks.

24/7 Field Support

Complete turnkey plant partnership including foundation engineering drawings, on-site commissioning, operator training, and lifetime spare parts backup.
