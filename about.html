Who We Are

Pioneering Sustainable Construction Solutions

Founded by Jayesh Panchal in 2022, Jee Engineers brings together nearly two decades of deep industry expertise. Having worked in the brick machinery manufacturing sector since 2006, Mr. Panchal established Jee Engineers to deliver state-of-the-art, high-compaction fly ash brick making machines engineered for durability and peak productivity.

With over 18 years of hands-on technical experience, our leadership has helped 100+ clients launch successful brick manufacturing plants across India. Our commitment to heavy-duty engineering, eco-friendly innovation, and full turnkey support sets us apart in the industry.
