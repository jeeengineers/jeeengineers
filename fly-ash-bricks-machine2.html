Frequently Asked Questions

JEE 4HV Automatic Machine FAQs

Everything you need to know about PLC automation, daily plant capacity, and warranty.

The JEE 4HV plant produces 10,000 to 12,000 bricks per day (700+ bricks/hour) with continuous PLC automation and heavy vibro-compaction.

The machine is driven by a 19 HP 3-phase electric motor running on 440V AC at 50Hz frequency. We recommend a 30 kVA power line connection.

The industrial PLC automation panel controls hydraulic pressure, feeder vibration timing, and block ejection automatically, reducing manual labor overheads by up to 60%.

Every JEE 4HV plant includes a 1-Year Manufacturer Warranty, free plant layout design, on-site commissioning by experienced engineers, and 24/7 technical support hotline.
