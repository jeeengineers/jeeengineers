Frequently Asked Questions

To get started with your new machine:

  1. Read the user manual thoroughly
  2. Complete the initial setup as per instructions
  3. Attend the training session provided by our team
  4. Start with a test run before full production

In case of a breakdown:

  1. Check the troubleshooting guide in your manual
  2. Contact our technical support team
  3. Provide the machine serial number and issue description
  4. Our team will guide you through the process
